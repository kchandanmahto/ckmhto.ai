# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and professional
environment for everyone who participates in this project.

We expect all contributors, maintainers and participants to communicate
constructively and treat each other with respect.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and professional
- Communicating clearly and constructively
- Welcoming different perspectives
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback
- Focusing on technical issues rather than individuals
- Respecting project boundaries and intellectual property
- Helping maintain a safe and inclusive environment

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Threats or intimidation
- Publishing private or sensitive information
- Unwanted sexual or offensive content
- Deliberate attempts to damage the project or its community
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
  in a professional technical environment

---

## Contribution Standards

When contributing to the repository:

1. Keep discussions relevant to the project.
2. Explain technical decisions clearly.
3. Prefer constructive criticism over personal criticism.
4. Respect maintainers and other contributors.
5. Do not submit malicious, intentionally harmful or deceptive changes.
6. Follow the project's security and contribution guidelines.

---

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it
